# 最小对立对卡

**怎么用**

1. 遮住右列答案，只读左列句子，说出这张卡的口令（这两句怎么分）。
2. 揭开答案列对一遍：两句答案的“形”不一样，说不出差别就是没分对。
3. 错的卡明天先练它，对的卡隔三天再回头；一次只练一张。

来源：句子全部取自本工具 134 题题库，只把 `______` 换成正确选项，其余一个词都没改。

---

## 一、时态与语态

### 01 一般过去时 ↔ 现在完成时

| 句子 | 答案 |
| --- | --- |
| Yesterday our class ______ the science museum to learn about ancient inventions. | visited |
| Jenny ______ the Science Museum twice so far this year, and she says the robot show is fantastic. | has been to |

**口令**：先看有没有 yesterday：只说过去某一次，别用 have done

### 02 现在完成时 ↔ 过去进行时

| 句子 | 答案 |
| --- | --- |
| Jenny ______ the library book since last Monday, so she must return it tomorrow. | has kept |
| While I ______ the science report last night, the lights suddenly went out. | was writing |

**口令**：看 since 还是 while：since 管到现在，while 只管那一刻

### 03 一般现在时 ↔ 被动语态（现在）

| 句子 | 答案 |
| --- | --- |
| Our volunteer group ______ old clothes to families in need every winter. | collects |
| The lunar eclipse ______ by millions of people in Guangzhou every year. | is watched |

**口令**：先问主语能不能自己做；句尾有 by 就是被动

### 04 一般将来时 ↔ 被动语态（将来）

| 句子 | 答案 |
| --- | --- |
| Look at the sky! It ______ rain soon, so let's move the plants inside. | is going to |
| A new science museum ______ in our district next year, and students can visit it free. | will be built |

**口令**：博物馆不会自己建：主语被建就写 will be done

### 05 被动语态（过去）↔ 被动语态（完成）

| 句子 | 答案 |
| --- | --- |
| The first thermometer ______ by a scientist more than 400 years ago, and it changed medicine. | was invented |
| Up to now, three new bridges ______ across the river to connect the two villages. | have been built |

**口令**：数到现在的用 have been done，只到过去用 was done

## 二、非谓语

### 06 动名词 ↔ 不定式（动词搭配）

| 句子 | 答案 |
| --- | --- |
| The boy practises ______ the piano for an hour after school, even on busy days. | playing |
| The teacher asked the students ______ their phones away during the lecture about Internet addiction. | to put |

**口令**：先认空前的动词：practise 接 doing，ask sb 接 to do

### 07 remember doing ↔ forget to do

| 句子 | 答案 |
| --- | --- |
| I clearly remember ______ the door, but now it is open, so I must go back and check. | locking |
| Don't forget ______ the windows before the storm comes, or the rain will get in. | to close |

**口令**：先问事情做了没：做过了接 doing，没做接 to do

### 08 make sb do ↔ be made to do

| 句子 | 答案 |
| --- | --- |
| Optimism makes both you and other people ______ pleased. | feel |
| In Wang Zhenyi's time, most girls were made ______ at home instead of going to school. | to stay |

**口令**：主动 make 后不带 to，改成被动再把 to 补回来

### 09 介词 + doing ↔ 表目的 to do

| 句子 | 答案 |
| --- | --- |
| By ______ the lunar eclipse herself, Wang Zhenyi proved that it was not a sign from heaven. | watching |
| Wang Zhenyi built a simple model of the lunar eclipse ______ the movement of the moon more clearly. | to explain |

**口令**：空前面是介词就填 doing；表“为了”就填 to do

### 10 be used to doing ↔ 不定式 to do

| 句子 | 答案 |
| --- | --- |
| Grandma is used to ______ up early, though life in the city is quite different. | getting |
| When you step into a new environment, you must have a strong wish ______ in. | to fit |

**口令**：先看 to 是介词还是不定式：be used to 后接 doing，wish 后接 to do

## 三、从句

### 11 if 从句 ↔ 主句（主将从现）

| 句子 | 答案 |
| --- | --- |
| In her opinion, if you don't talk to someone about your problems, you ______ worse. | will feel |
| If the rain ______ tomorrow, we will visit the science museum with our foreign friends. | stops |

**口令**：看空在主句还是 if 从句：从句用现在，主句才用 will

### 12 宾语从句语序 ↔ 特殊疑问句

| 句子 | 答案 |
| --- | --- |
| Could you tell me ______ the science museum tomorrow? | when we will visit |
| —______ will the new library be open to students? —In about two weeks, I think. | How soon |

**口令**：看是不是问句：问句把助动词提前，从句不提前

### 13 定语从句 ↔ 名词性从句

| 句子 | 答案 |
| --- | --- |
| A few years ago, the young man ______ travelled on the same road decided to visit the family. | who |
| ______ the ancient people believed about the eclipse turned out to be wrong. | What |

**口令**：空后句子完整就用 who/which，缺宾语才用 what

### 14 让步状语从句 ↔ 原因连词

| 句子 | 答案 |
| --- | --- |
| ______ her life was short, Wang Zhenyi still wrote many important books about the stars. | Although |
| Wang Zhenyi kept studying the stars ______ she was very interested in them. | because |

**口令**：前后意思相反填 although，后句讲原因填 because

### 15 宾语从句时态：客观真理 ↔ 时态呼应

| 句子 | 答案 |
| --- | --- |
| In class, our science teacher told us that light ______ much faster than sound. | travels |
| The head teacher announced that the school ______ a science show the next Friday. | would hold |

**口令**：从句讲客观真理不退时态，讲将来就退成 would

## 四、词法

### 16 冠词 an ↔ the

| 句子 | 答案 |
| --- | --- |
| It took Archimedes less than ______ hour to solve the king's problem. | an |
| Her model was ______ first one that could show a lunar eclipse clearly. | the |

**口令**：空后元音音素开头用 an，后接序数词用 the

### 17 代词 her ↔ hers

| 句子 | 答案 |
| --- | --- |
| Wang Zhenyi's father supported ______ when she wanted to study the stars. | her |
| This notebook isn't mine. It's ______, because her name is on the first page. | hers |

**口令**：空后有名词用 her，空后没名词用 hers

### 18 原级 as…as ↔ 比较级 than

| 句子 | 答案 |
| --- | --- |
| The new model of the lunar eclipse is as ______ as the old one, and it is easier to carry. | beautiful |
| Developments in technology now tie us ______ than ever before. | closer |

**口令**：as … as 中间填原级，than 前面填比较级

### 19 形容词 ↔ 副词

| 句子 | 答案 |
| --- | --- |
| After cleaning the whole house alone, my mother looked ______ and went to bed early. | tired |
| Nancy plays the piano very ______, and she dreams of becoming a musician. | well |

**口令**：空说的是主语状态填形容词，修饰动作填副词

### 20 不定代词 neither ↔ others

| 句子 | 答案 |
| --- | --- |
| My parents are both busy, so ______ of them can take me to the museum this weekend. | neither |
| And expecting ______ to be perfect can add to your stress level too. | others |

**口令**：两个都不 → neither；泛指别的人 → others
